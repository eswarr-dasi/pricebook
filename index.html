Honesty about the numbers

Nutrition and ingredients come from Open Food Facts, a community database under the ODbL licence, and can be wrong or out of date. Nutri-Score and NOVA are third party classifications shown as published, not our verdict. The fullness estimate is an estimate built from four visible inputs and is not medical or dietary guidance.

Custom food

Tare

Tare is the button on a grocery scale that subtracts the container. This app does the same to a price tag.

  • Scan or search. Barcode, digits, or the product name.
  • Type the shelf price. The app remembers your lowest, per item, per store.
  • Log it if you want. Targets are yours to set, or to ignore entirely.

No account. No ads. Everything stays on this device.
