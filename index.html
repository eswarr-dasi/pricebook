What this app does not do

  • No good or bad food labels. Numbers only, you decide.
  • No weight goals, no calorie targets, no streaks.
  • No account required. No ads, no affiliate links, no brand influence.
  • Works offline in the aisle.
  • Your price history stays on this device.

Honesty about the numbers

Nutrition and ingredients come from Open Food Facts, a community database under the ODbL licence, and can be wrong or out of date. Nutri-Score and NOVA are third party classifications shown as published, not our verdict. The fullness estimate is an estimate built from four visible inputs and is not medical or dietary guidance.
